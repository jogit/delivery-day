Delivery DayFrançais

Stop refreshing the Tesla app.

Delivery Day watches your Tesla order and notifies you only when something matters: VIN assigned, delivery window changed, appointment, amount due, trade-in offer.

Download the APKView on GitHub
  • Delivery windowCountdown and timeline up to delivery day.
  • Smart notificationsReadable, only for real changes. Hidden on the lock screen.
  • Change historySee exactly what changed and when.
  • Payment & trade-inAmount due and trade-in offer at a glance.
  • Tesla tasksKnow when Tesla asks you to do something.
  • Home screen widgetYour delivery date without opening the app.

Install

  1. Download the latest APK from GitHub Releases and open it on your phone.
  2. Tap “Sign in with your Tesla account” and sign in on tesla.com.
  3. Allow notifications and set battery usage to Unrestricted (Samsung/Xiaomi).
  4. Optional: add the widget.

Private by design

No server, no ads, no analytics. You sign in on tesla.com (OAuth + PKCE): your password never reaches the app. Tokens are encrypted with the Android Keystore and order data stays on your phone.
